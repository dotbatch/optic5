What a small business website has to do

Most visitors arrive on a phone with one question: can you solve my problem, and how do I reach you? A good site answers that in seconds. Awards and animations don’t pay the bills. Calls, bookings and form submissions do.

What we build

  • Custom design. Your site looks like your business, not a template with the logo swapped.
  • Mobile first. We design for the phone in someone’s hand, then scale up.
  • Speed. Pages stay light, because slow sites lose visitors and hurt search performance.
  • Clear calls to action. Calling, booking or asking for a quote is always one step away.
  • Copy that sounds like you. We help with wording that’s plain and specific.
  • Editing you can manage. WordPress or a custom build, depending on how often you’ll change things.

Built for local search

When someone searches for what you do in Austin, Google looks at whether your site is relevant, whether your business details are consistent across the web, and whether people trust you. So we set up:

  • A clear page for each thing you sell, written for what customers actually search
  • Accurate titles, descriptions and structured data for each page
  • Consistent business name, phone number and service area
  • A connection to your Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics
  • Redirects from your old pages, so you keep whatever search value they’ve built

We won’t promise a number one ranking. Nobody honest can, and anyone who does is a reason to be careful. What we can promise is a site that gives you a fair shot.

Forms that do something

A contact form that just sends an email is a missed chance. Yours can route the inquiry to the right person, send an instant reply, add the lead to your CRM and start your follow-up automation. That’s where our web and automation work meet.

Common questions

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We look at what’s working first, keep what’s earned its place, and redirect old addresses so you don’t lose visitors or search history in the move.

Yes. Your domain, content and files are yours, and we’ll hand them over if you ever decide to move on.

Can you host it too?

Yes, though you don’t have to. See our managed web hosting if you’d like one team handling it all.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, custom features and how much content needs writing. After a first conversation you’ll get a clear quote before anything starts.
