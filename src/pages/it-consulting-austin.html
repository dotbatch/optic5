Who this is for

You’ve reached the size where technology decisions matter, but not the size where a full-time technology person makes sense. So decisions get made by whoever’s most comfortable with computers, or by the salesperson who called last. We give you someone on your side of the table.

What we help with

Choosing software

We work out what you actually need, compare the realistic options and help you avoid long contracts for features you won’t use. If you’ve been quoted something, we’ll read it with you and tell you what it means in plain English.

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace

Setup, clean-up, shared files that people can actually find, and proper handling when someone joins or leaves the company.

Security basics

Most small business security problems come from a few fixable gaps. We start with the ones that matter most:

  • Multi-factor authentication on email and financial accounts
  • A password manager, so nobody shares logins on sticky notes
  • Tested backups of the data you can’t lose
  • Shutting off access when staff leave
  • Teaching your team to spot fake invoices and login pages

AI decisions

Should you pay for that AI add-on? Is it safe to put customer data in that tool? We’ll give you a straight answer, and help you set simple rules with our AI policy template.

Dealing with vendors

When something goes wrong between your phone provider, your software company and your internet service, each will blame the others. We can make the calls and sort it out.

What we don’t do

We’ll be upfront when you need a specialist, such as an accountant, a lawyer, or a security firm for a formal audit or an active breach. We’d rather point you to the right people than guess.
