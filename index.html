FAQ

Common questions

We're a small team and not very technical. Is AI really for us?

Yes. Small teams usually benefit the most, because every hour saved matters. We handle the technical side and train your team on simple, everyday use. If a tool won't clearly save you time or money, we'll tell you.

Do we have to switch software?

Usually not. We start with what you already use, like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, your CRM, or your scheduling app, and connect them. We only recommend new tools when there's a clear payoff.

Is our customer data safe with AI tools?

We pick business-grade AI services with clear data-handling terms, limit what each automation can access, and put human approval on anything customer-facing. We'll also write a simple AI policy for your staff.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Most clients start with a fixed-price Quick Start project or a small monthly retainer. You'll get a clear quote after the free assessment, with no surprise invoices.

Do you still build and host websites?

Absolutely. Web design, development and managed hosting are where Optic5 started. Today we build sites that do more, with smart forms, chat, and booking tied straight into your workflows.

Do you work outside Austin?

We're based in Austin and serve the whole Central Texas region in person, from Georgetown to San Marcos. We also work remotely with businesses anywhere in Texas and the US.
