08 — FAQ

Good questions

We’re a small team and not very technical. Is AI for us?

Often, yes. Small teams feel every hour they get back. We handle the technical side and keep the day-to-day simple. If a tool won’t clearly save you time or money, we’ll tell you.

Do we have to switch software?

Usually not. We start with what you already use and connect it. We only suggest something new when the payoff is clear.

Is our customer data safe with AI tools?

We choose business-grade services with clear data terms, limit what each automation can access, and can require human approval on anything customer-facing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the work. After a first conversation you’ll get a clear quote, so you know the cost before anything starts.

Do you still build and host websites?

Yes. Websites, hosting and consulting are where Optic5 started, and they’re still a core part of what we do.

Do you work outside Austin?

We’re based in Austin and work in person across Central Texas. We can also work remotely with businesses elsewhere.
